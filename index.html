SONARFALL

You can't see the maze. You can only listen for it.

Our first game. The walls are invisible until a sonar ping washes over them, and then they fade again. You get a few pings and a ticking clock, and you find the exit from what you remember.

Get it on Google Play GitHub

Built with zero art or sound files. Everything is generated in code.

01 · PING

Ping to see

The walls are invisible until a sonar ping crosses them. Pings are scarce, so every one is a decision.

02 · REMEMBER

Remember the way

The walls fade back into the dark. You have 34 seconds and three stars to drag your way to the exit from memory.

03 · SURVIVE

Clear it flawless

Decoys show up from level 3 and the exit starts moving at level 6. Hidden bonus echoes win you back a star.

04 · CONTINUE

Keep going

Every maze is generated fresh and the levels never end. Your progress is saved, so you pick up right where you left off.
